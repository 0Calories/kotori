# Kotori

Kotori helps intermediate and advanced learners sound natural in Japanese or English. It pairs a bilingual dictionary that says how words are really used with a coach for whole sentences and a deck for spaced-repetition study.

## Language

### Learners and languages

**Learner**:
A person studying with Kotori; also called a user.
_Avoid_: Student

**English learner**:
A learner studying English, explained in Japanese.
_Avoid_: Learner of English, Japanese speaker learning English, Japanese learner (for someone studying English)

**Japanese learner**:
A learner studying Japanese, explained in English.
_Avoid_: Learner of Japanese, English speaker learning Japanese, English learner (for someone studying Japanese)

**Account**:
A user's sign-up, which is pending until an admin approves it.
_Avoid_: Registration

**Profile**:
A user's language direction, display name, time zone and daily study limits.
_Avoid_: Settings, preferences

**Admin**:
A person who approves accounts and reviews proposals.
_Avoid_: Moderator, reviewer

**Native language**:
The language a learner already speaks, in which everything is explained to them.
_Avoid_: Source language, interface language, L1

**Target language**:
The language a learner is studying.
_Avoid_: Foreign language, learning language, L2

**Direction**:
One of the two pairings Kotori teaches: a Japanese learner or an English learner.
_Avoid_: Mode, language pair, locale

### Dictionary

**Entry**:
One word or expression in the dictionary, in one language and explained in the other.
_Avoid_: Term, lemma, item

**Headword**:
The written form an entry is listed under.
_Avoid_: Title, word, lemma

**Reading**:
How a headword is pronounced, written out: kana for a Japanese entry, IPA for an English one.
_Avoid_: Pronunciation, phonetics

**Form**:
One of the ways an entry is written: a kanji or kana writing of a Japanese entry, a spelling or inflection of an English one.
_Avoid_: Variant, writing

**Sense**:
One distinct meaning of an entry.
_Avoid_: Meaning, definition

**Gloss**:
A short rendering of a sense in the other language; a sense has one or more.
_Avoid_: Translation, definition, meaning

**Tag**:
A seed dictionary's own code for a grammatical or subject fact about a sense or form, such as its part of speech or field.
_Avoid_: Label, code

**Example**:
A sentence showing a sense in use, in Japanese and English.
_Avoid_: Sample, example sentence pair

**Seed**:
An outside data set the dictionary is built from: JMDict, the Wiktionaries, Tatoeba, subtitle word counts.
_Avoid_: Import, dataset, upstream

**English dictionary**:
The entries for English words, explained in Japanese, that Kotori assembles from several seeds for English learners.
_Avoid_: Eiwa, reverse dictionary

**Source**:
Where a dictionary fact came from: a named seed, Kotori, or a user.
_Avoid_: Origin, provenance, author

**Kotori** (as a source):
Kotori's own AI inference, always marked apart from what a seed states.
_Avoid_: AI, the model, generated

**Fill**:
A fact Kotori infers where the seeds are silent; a fill never overwrites what a seed states.
_Avoid_: Override, guess, backfill

**Enrichment**:
Adding to an entry what the seeds lack, such as natural examples and audio.
_Avoid_: Augmentation, generation, processing

### Usage

**Register**:
The social setting a word or sentence fits: how formal it is, who says it, and to whom.
_Avoid_: Tone, politeness, style

**Formality**:
The axis of register running from vulgar through casual, neutral and polite to formal.
_Avoid_: Politeness level, register (for this one axis)

**Label**:
A coded usage fact about a sense that a learner would not assume unprompted, such as colloquial, honorific, dated or British.
_Avoid_: Tag, nuance, judgment, chip

**Severity**:
How loudly a label speaks to the learner: avoid, caution, info or quiet.
_Avoid_: Level, importance, priority

**Usage pattern**:
A short statement of how a sense is used, such as "usually to one's subordinate" or "usually in the negative".
_Avoid_: Pattern, usage note, sense note, hint

**Common mistake**:
One short teaching about something learners often get wrong: a surprise in an entry or one of its senses that the labels don't already explain, or a tempting but unnatural way of saying what the learner asked the Coach for.
_Avoid_: Pitfall, warning, caution, trap

**False friend**:
A common mistake on an English entry whose katakana loan has drifted in meaning, misleading a Japanese speaker.
_Avoid_: Loan trap, katakana trap

**Loan link**:
The pairing of a katakana word with the English word it was borrowed from, and whether the meaning stayed the same or diverged.
_Avoid_: Loan word, loanword match, cognate

**Usage evidence**:
What the seeds show about whether an entry is actually used, enough to call it common or rarely heard.
_Avoid_: Frequency, popularity, stats

**Familiarity**:
A 1–5 rating of how familiar an entry is to native speakers today.
_Avoid_: Frequency, commonness, difficulty

**Similar word**:
An entry close in meaning to a sense, shown with its difference.
_Avoid_: Synonym, related word

**Difference**:
How a similar word departs from the sense beside it, such as more casual, the respectful form, or the British word.
_Avoid_: Contrast, comparison, distinction

### Ask Kotori

**Ask**:
A learner's request for a word or expression the dictionary lacks.
_Avoid_: Request, lookup, question

**Proposal**:
Kotori's one answer to an asked-for word, shared by every learner who asks for it.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, submission, request

**Draft**:
The entry Kotori writes for a proposal, shown as unverified only to the learners who asked.
_Avoid_: Proposed entry, unverified entry, candidate

**Route**:
What kind of input an ask turns out to be: a lookup, a sentence for the Coach, a name, the wrong language, or nonsense.
_Avoid_: Triage, classification, intent

**Linked**:
A proposal that names an entry the dictionary already has.
_Avoid_: Alias, duplicate, matched

**Rejected**:
A proposal Kotori judged not to be a real expression.
_Avoid_: Discarded, declined, invalid

**Approved**:
A proposal whose draft an admin made a real entry.
_Avoid_: Accepted, published, verified

**Discarded**:
A proposal whose draft an admin turned down.
_Avoid_: Rejected, deleted, dismissed

### Coach

**Coach**:
The part of Kotori that works on whole sentences rather than dictionary entries.
_Avoid_: Tutor, assistant, AI chat

**Naturalness check**:
The Coach's reading of a sentence the learner wrote in their target language: how it comes across to a native speaker, and more natural ways to say it.
_Avoid_: Grammar check, correction, proofreading

**How do I say**:
The Coach's answer to something the learner describes in their native language: the ways native speakers actually say it.
_Avoid_: Translation, phrasebook

### Study

**Note**:
Something a learner saved to study: one sense of an entry, or a phrase the Coach gave or the learner typed. Its content is fixed when saved and does not follow later changes to the dictionary.
_Avoid_: Card, saved entry, saved item, flashcard

**Deck**:
A group of notes a learner reviews together.
_Avoid_: Collection, list, library

**Review**:
One answer to one note.
_Avoid_: Attempt, quiz, repetition

**Rating**:
The learner's verdict on a review: again, hard, good or easy.
_Avoid_: Grade, score, answer

**Due notes**:
The notes a learner has to review now, in the order they will be shown.
_Avoid_: Queue, review queue, due cards

**Retention stage**:
How far along a note is in being learned: new, learning, review or relearning.
_Avoid_: Queue, stage, state, status

**Lapse**:
Forgetting a note that had already left learning.
_Avoid_: Failure, miss

**Leech**:
A note that has lapsed so often that reviewing it again is not working.
_Avoid_: Problem note, hard card

**Study day**:
A learner's day of study, which rolls over at 4am in their own time zone, not at midnight.
_Avoid_: Calendar day, date

**Streak**:
The run of consecutive study days on which a learner reviewed.
_Avoid_: Chain, run

### Audio

**Clip**:
A spoken recording of a headword or an example.
_Avoid_: Audio file, recording, TTS

**Voice**:
A named speaker Kotori can record a clip with, known by its language, gender, age and manner.
_Avoid_: Speaker, narrator

**Cast**:
The set of voices available for a language.
_Avoid_: Voice list, voice pool

**Casting**:
Choosing the voice that fits who would say a sentence, and how.
_Avoid_: Voice selection, voice matching
