# Kotori

Kotori helps intermediate and advanced learners sound natural in Japanese or English. It pairs a dictionary grounded in how native speakers actually use words with Ask Kotori for questions, phrasing, and naturalness.

## Language

### Learners

**English learner**:
Anyone studying English in Kotori, with explanations in Japanese.
_Avoid_: Learner of English, Japanese speaker learning English, Japanese learner (for someone studying English)

**Japanese learner**:
Anyone studying Japanese in Kotori, with explanations in English.
_Avoid_: Learner of Japanese, English speaker learning Japanese, English learner (for someone studying Japanese)

### Dictionary

**Entry**:
A single dictionary entry with an English facet, a Japanese facet, or both when two expressions pair as counterparts; each facet holds that language's forms, senses, usage, and cautions.
_Avoid_: Term, lemma, item

**Form**:
One of the written shapes an entry takes across script, spelling, or grammatical inflection, such as kanji, kana, spelling variants, or conjugations.
_Avoid_: Reading, headword, variant, writing

**Sense**:
One distinct meaning of an entry.
_Avoid_: Meaning, definition

**Gloss**:
A short rendering of a sense in the other language; a sense has one or more.
_Avoid_: Translation, definition, meaning

**Source**:
Where a dictionary entry or fact came from, such as an external dataset (JMDict, Wiktionary, Tatoeba, subtitle corpora), Kotori, or a user.
_Avoid_: Seed, provenance, dataset, upstream, Eiwa

**Enrichment**:
Adding to an entry what external sources lack, such as usage context, cautions, examples, and audio.
_Avoid_: Augmentation, generation, processing

**Usage**:
How, where, and by whom an expression is naturally used, covering formality, medium, speaker demographics, region, and phrasing patterns.
_Avoid_: Register, tone, style, nuance

**True loanword**:
A word imported from another language that keeps the exact same meaning as in its origin language.
_Avoid_: Same-meaning loan

**Wasei-eigo**:
A Japanese word or expression built from English words or roots that either does not exist in standard English or has taken on a completely different meaning in Japan.
_Avoid_: False friend, loan trap, katakana trap

**Origin**:
The language (and original word) a loanword or wasei-eigo came from, such as French for ズボン or English for コンビニ.
_Avoid_: Etymology, donor language

**Caution**:
A warning about a common mistake, pitfall, or formality or demographic trap when using an expression.
_Avoid_: Pitfall, trap, common mistake

**Frequency**:
How commonly native speakers use and encounter an expression today, weighed across multiple signals rather than raw corpus count alone.
_Avoid_: Familiarity, commonness, raw frequency, difficulty

### Ask Kotori

**Ask Kotori**:
Kotori's conversational AI feature for finding dictionary entries, answering language questions, suggesting natural ways to say something, and checking whether phrasing sounds natural.
_Avoid_: Coach, tutor, assistant, AI chat

**Conversation**:
A learner's private thread with Ask Kotori, in one target language, where each follow-up builds on the answers before it.
_Avoid_: Chat, thread, session, ask

**Turn**:
One message from the learner and Ask Kotori's answer to it.
_Avoid_: Exchange, round, message

**Drafted entry**:
An entry Ask Kotori wrote that the admin hasn't reviewed yet; once approved it is an ordinary entry, and once discarded it is never shown to anyone new.
_Avoid_: Proposal, draft, suggestion, AI entry

### Audio

**Voice**:
A named persona Kotori synthesizes speech with, characterized by language, gender, age, and speaking style.
_Avoid_: Speaker, narrator
