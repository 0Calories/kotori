# Kotori

Kotori helps intermediate and advanced learners sound natural in Japanese or English. It pairs a bilingual dictionary grounded in how native speakers actually use words with a coach for whole sentences.

## Language

### Learners and languages

**User**:
Anyone with an account in Kotori, whether a learner or an admin.
_Avoid_: Member

**Learner**:
A user studying a language with Kotori.
_Avoid_: Student

**English learner**:
A learner studying English, explained in Japanese.
_Avoid_: Learner of English, Japanese speaker learning English, Japanese learner (for someone studying English)

**Japanese learner**:
A learner studying Japanese, explained in English.
_Avoid_: Learner of Japanese, English speaker learning Japanese, English learner (for someone studying Japanese)

**Account**:
A user's identity and access status in Kotori, pending until an admin approves it.
_Avoid_: Registration

**Profile**:
A learner's language direction, display name, time zone, and daily limits.
_Avoid_: Settings, preferences

**Admin**:
A user who approves accounts and reviews proposals.
_Avoid_: Moderator, reviewer

**Native language**:
The language a learner already speaks, in which everything is explained to them.
_Avoid_: Source language, interface language, L1

**Target language**:
The language a learner is studying.
_Avoid_: Foreign language, learning language, L2

**Direction**:
One of the two pairings Kotori teaches: a Japanese learner (native English, target Japanese) or an English learner (native Japanese, target English).
_Avoid_: Mode, language pair, locale

### Dictionary

**Entry**:
One word or expression in the dictionary, in one language and explained in the other.
_Avoid_: Term, lemma, item

**Headword**:
The written form an entry is listed under.
_Avoid_: Title, word, lemma

**Reading**:
How a headword is pronounced, written out: kana for a Japanese entry, IPA for an English one.
_Avoid_: Pronunciation, phonetics

**Form**:
One of the ways an entry is written: a kanji or kana writing of a Japanese entry, or a spelling or inflection of an English one.
_Avoid_: Variant, writing

**Sense**:
One distinct meaning of an entry.
_Avoid_: Meaning, definition

**Gloss**:
A short rendering of a sense in the other language; a sense has one or more.
_Avoid_: Translation, definition, meaning

**Tag**:
A seed's raw code for a grammatical or domain fact on a sense or form, such as part of speech or subject field.
_Avoid_: Code

**Example**:
A sentence pair in Japanese and English showing a sense in natural use.
_Avoid_: Sample, sentence pair

**Seed**:
An external data set the dictionary is built from, such as JMDict, Wiktionary, Tatoeba, or subtitle word counts.
_Avoid_: Import, dataset, upstream, Eiwa

**Source**:
Where a dictionary fact came from: a named seed, Kotori (AI inference), or a user.
_Avoid_: Origin, provenance, author

**Enrichment**:
Adding to an entry what the seeds lack, such as usage judgments, examples, and clips.
_Avoid_: Augmentation, generation, processing

### Usage

**Register**:
The social setting a word or sentence fits: how formal it is, who says it, and to whom.
_Avoid_: Tone, politeness, style

**Formality**:
The axis of register running from vulgar through casual, neutral, and polite to formal.
_Avoid_: Politeness level, register (for this single axis)

**Label**:
A usage fact surfaced on a sense that a learner would not assume unprompted, such as casual, honorific, dated, or British.
_Avoid_: Tag (for surfaced usage badges), chip

**Usage pattern**:
A short statement of how a sense is used in practice, such as "usually to one's subordinate" or "usually in the negative".
_Avoid_: Pattern

**Common mistake**:
A tempting word choice or phrasing that sounds unnatural or means something else to native speakers, paired with why.
_Avoid_: Pitfall, trap

**False friend**:
A common mistake on an English entry whose katakana loan has drifted in meaning, misleading an English learner.
_Avoid_: Loan trap, katakana trap

**Familiarity**:
How familiar an expression is to native speakers today, as distinct from raw corpus frequency.
_Avoid_: Frequency, commonness, difficulty

**Similar word**:
An entry close in meaning to a sense, paired with how it differs in register, region, or usage.
_Avoid_: Synonym, related word

### Ask Kotori

**Ask**:
A learner's query for a word or expression the dictionary search could not answer.
_Avoid_: Request, question

**Route**:
What kind of input an ask turns out to be before drafting: a lookup, a sentence for the Coach, a proper name, the wrong language, or nonsense.
_Avoid_: Triage, classification, intent

**Proposal**:
The shared resolution of a normalized ask: linked to an existing entry, rejected as not a real expression, or pending admin review until approved (as a new entry or form) or discarded.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, submission

**Draft**:
The unverified entry Kotori writes for a pending proposal.
_Avoid_: Proposed entry, candidate

### Coach

**Coach**:
The part of Kotori that helps learners write and phrase whole sentences rather than look up entries.
_Avoid_: Tutor, assistant, AI chat

**Naturalness check**:
The Coach's reading of a target-language sentence the learner wrote: how it comes across to native speakers, what to fix, and natural rewrites.
_Avoid_: Grammar check, correction, proofreading

**How do I say**:
The Coach's idiomatic target-language ways of expressing an idea the learner described in their native language.
_Avoid_: Translation, phrasebook

### Audio

**Clip**:
A synthesized speech recording of a headword, example, phrase, or gloss.
_Avoid_: Audio file, recording, TTS

**Voice**:
A named persona Kotori synthesizes clips with, characterized by language, gender, age, and speaking style.
_Avoid_: Speaker, narrator

**Casting**:
Choosing which voice fits who would say an example sentence and how.
_Avoid_: Voice selection, voice matching
