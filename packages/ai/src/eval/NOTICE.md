# The golden sets' dictionary excerpts and licence

`goldenForm.ts`, `goldenPitfalls.ts` and `goldenLoans.ts` quote dictionary entries so the evals
see words as the offline passes do: glosses, parts of speech, usage tags and notes. Unlike the
rest of this repository, which is under the MIT licence in `/LICENSE`, those quoted excerpts are
adaptations of works under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence,
and are shared under that licence: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>.

- **JMdict**, © the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group, used in conformance
  with the Group's licence (CC BY-SA 4.0): <https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html>,
  <https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html>. The Japanese words, their glosses, tags, notes
  and loanword sources (JMdict created 2026-10-27).
- **English Wiktionary** (<https://en.wiktionary.org>) and **Japanese Wiktionary**
  (<https://ja.wiktionary.org>), by their contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0, as extracted by
  Wiktextract (<https://github.com/tatuylonen/wiktextract>) for kaikki.org. The English words'
  Japanese glosses and notes, from the curated file `data/eiwa/enwiktionary-ja.jsonl` (see
  `data/eiwa/NOTICE.md`).

Changes: entries are cut to a few senses and reduced to their glosses, parts of speech, label
codes and notes. The expected answers, evidence notes and the code are Kotori's own (MIT).
